# Piggybank — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: 2026-03-15

## Summary

Piggybank collects **zero** data. Everything stays on your device.

## Data Storage

- All account and transaction data is stored in a local SQLite database
  on your device only.
- No data is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.
- No data is shared with Apple, the developer, or any other entity.

## Data Collection

Piggybank does **not** collect, store, or transmit:
- Personal information (names, emails, phone numbers)
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, or hardware identifiers)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports
- Advertising identifiers

## Third-Party Services

Piggybank uses **no** third-party services:
- No analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude)
- No crash reporting (no Sentry, no Crashlytics)
- No advertising networks
- No social login providers
- No cloud databases or sync services

## Network Access

Piggybank makes **zero** network requests. The app functions entirely
offline. No internet connection is required at any time.

## Data Deletion

- Uninstalling Piggybank permanently deletes all data.
- There is no backup, sync, or recovery mechanism.
- Data cannot be recovered after deletion.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Piggybank is designed for use by parents/guardians managing children's
allowances. The app:
- Does not collect any information from children
- Does not require account creation
- Does not connect to the internet
- Stores only locally-entered financial simulation data

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
